Books for Discrete Math

Required Books

We will be using two free online textbooks this semester:

  • Discrete Mathematics: An Open Introduction (DM), 4th edition, by Oscar Levin
  • Combinatorics Through Guided Discovery (CTGD), by Kenneth Bogart

Note: The DM textbook is a new edition. If you experience issues with its interactive features, please use the previous edition.

Recommended Book

An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics by Clive Newstead
A free, open-source introduction to proof writing and pure mathematics. Particularly useful for logic, sets, functions, induction, relations, number theory, and combinatorics.
