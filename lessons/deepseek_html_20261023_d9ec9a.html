Ordered vs. unordered pairs

Count ordered pairs \((i,j)\) with \(1\le i

  • Choose the unordered pair first, then order it: \(\binom n2\cdot 1\).
  • Choose \(i\) (\(n\) ways), then \(j\) (\(n-1\) ways), then divide by 2 since each unordered pair was counted twice: \(\frac{n(n-1)}2\).

Hence \(\binom n2=\frac{n(n-1)}2\) — the same identity you met in Lesson 12's practice set, now proved by double counting.
